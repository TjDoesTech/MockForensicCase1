<h2>Description</h2>
Jake Manson is currently a 26 year old, who in the past has had previous minor offenses
like theft and drug charges. Jake was recently arrested for trafficking and possession of
illegally purchased and stole guns. Jake was given a plea bargain so he would not face any
jail time and was instead placed on parole. One of the stipulations for his parole states that
he may not use a computer to engage in any type of activity that pertains to the selling or
buying of guns. He was also warned that he should not even visit gun related websites as it
would make his parole officer suspicious. During a recent visit Jake had with his parole officer, forensic triage software was used to
look at his hard drive. The parole officer found images of firearms and as such she thought
Jake was getting involved with the buying and selling of weapons again and reported it to
supervisors. Jake wanted to contest the findings. The court hired me to investigate this
matter. I was given permission to search through Jake’s hard drive to find information
pertaining to the buying and selling of guns. The court has asked for detailed reporting of all
Jake’s computer activity. This report will summarize the findings I have found regarding
Jake’s web and computer activity and will have objective findings with evidence to back up
those findings.

<h2>Executive Summary:</h2>
On the hard drive of Jake Manson, evidence relating to firearms was found throughout the
hard drive. For detailed information, locate the appropriate section inside of this report,
this is a summary of the findings related to firearms inside of hard drive: <br />

- Inside of the My Documents folder on the hard drive, there is a folder called
“Merchandise” with images of firearms inside of it. These files were all put
there on September 26, 2005. There was also a sales spreadsheet and a
document called “Things to do.doc”. The sales spreadsheet contained sale
transactions of firearms and the details about those sales. The to do list
document mentions buying ammo and picking up a gun safe among other
things.

- Inside Jake’s User profile, the Folder and Files mentioned above were copied
to his own personal My Documents on the PC. The files all have different
creation times from when they were put onto his user profile, on October 31,2005.
- The only file to have its contents modified in any way was the sales
document and a name as well as a date were added to a transaction that
was missing them.

- Jake’s recent folder had shortcut files created that displayed he had recently
opened all the images in the merchandise folder as well as the sales and
things to do document. The other profile on the computer, Marilyn, was also
looked at but inside of her recent folders and desktop there was nothing
pertaining to firearms.

- There is an email exchange between Jake, and someone named Edgar Zed on
the date of September 26, 2005. In this exchange Edgar told Jake he wanted
3 to make some deals because he needed money soon. Jake responded to the
email saying he would send some pics of merchandise soon.

- The internet History files show that the images inside of the merchandise
folder were gotten from different websites related to information on the
related firearms, on September 26. 2005. There is a gap between the end of
September and the end of October when the computer was browsed on
next. On October 31, 2005, Jake visited websites that talked about gun laws
as well as the laws of guns in the state of Pennsylvania. There was also a
google search for “gun pictures” as well as “fn five seven”. He also looked up
information about the laws regarding selling firearms inside Pennsylvania.
He was doing research on the laws regarding firearms, but no new email
exchanges were made during this time, The sales spreadsheet also did not
have any new sales added to it.
